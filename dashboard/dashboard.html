          How a detection gets here

          Camera frames are processed every 5th frame. YOLOv8 finds the plate, EasyOCR reads it, and the two scores combine into one confidence.

          Valid Indian-format plates at ≥ 0.50 are posted to POST /api/detections and stored in PostgreSQL. A repeat of the same plate at the same camera within 30 seconds returns the existing record instead of saving a copy.

          Dashboard reads: GET /api/analytics/summary, /hourly, /daily, /cameras for stats & chart, GET /api/vehicles/{plate}/trajectory for tracking, and GET / POST /api/alerts + PATCH /api/alerts/{id} for alerts.
